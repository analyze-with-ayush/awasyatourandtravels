Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, routes, and chauffeur service.

Can I book a taxi to any city in India from Indore, Bhopal or Ujjain? expand_more

Yes! All our commercial vehicles possess All India Tourist Permits (AITP). We regularly operate trips from Indore, Bhopal, and Ujjain to any destination across India, including Rajasthan (Jaipur, Udaipur, Kota), Gujarat (Ahmedabad, Surat, Statue of Unity), Maharashtra (Mumbai, Shirdi, Pune), Delhi NCR, and beyond.

How is the fare determined for outstation trips? expand_more

Our pricing is handled directly by the owner to ensure the best customized rate with zero middleman commissions. Share your trip date, pickup point, destination, and vehicle choice on WhatsApp or Call, and we will quote the most competitive, upfront all-inclusive rate.

Can I book a one-way drop between Indore, Bhopal and Ujjain? expand_more

Yes! We provide dedicated one-way drops as well as round trips. One-way drops cover single-leg travel without any return fare obligations, making it economical for flight transit, university students, and business visits.

What happens if my flight arrives late at Indore Airport? expand_more

When you share your flight number during booking, we monitor flight delays in real-time. Your chauffeur will automatically adjust arrival timing and wait for you at the airport arrivals gate with zero waiting stress.

How do I confirm my booking and what payment modes are accepted? expand_more

Simply share your trip date, pickup point, and destination on WhatsApp or call 99265 47855. Payment is accepted via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, or Cash. GST invoices are provided promptly for business travelers.
